The gap Dresscode owns

Not another image generator. Not another order book.

Visual AI tools create pictures. Tailor software stores records. Dresscode connects the most valuable moment between them: turning a client’s idea into a design they understand, approve and pay to produce.

1

Explore without wasting fabric

Start from words, a sketch, a garment photo or a textile direction. Test the look on the client and make focused changes before committing to a sample.

2

Turn discussion into agreement

Save clear versions, share the studio’s branded approval page and keep the chosen direction attached to the consultation rather than buried in chat messages.

3

Hand the decision to production

Carry the approved visual, measurements, quote, deposit, due date and notes into a printable work order the tailoring team can actually use.
